| ------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FR-001  | Functional  | The system shall allow students to view active placement and internship drives and submit applications for drives for which they are eligible.              | High     | Pass: Eligible students can successfully apply to an active drive. Fail: The system allows an ineligible student to apply.                                         | Enables students to participate in relevant placement and internship opportunities. |
| FR-002  | Functional  | The system shall parse uploaded student resumes in PDF format and extract relevant skill tags from the resume.                                              | High     | Pass: A valid PDF resume is processed and relevant skills are extracted. Fail: The system fails to extract available skill information.                            | Automates resume processing and provides information required for profile matching. |
| FR-003  | Functional  | The system shall evaluate student profiles against the eligibility criteria defined for each active company drive, including CGPA and backlog requirements. | High     | Pass: Eligible and ineligible students are correctly identified with discrepancy reasons. Fail: An applicant who does not satisfy the criteria is marked eligible. | Ensures only students satisfying company requirements proceed.                      |
| FR-004  | Functional  | The system shall manage multi-round interview schedules and maintain queues of students for each interview round.                                           | High     | Pass: Students are assigned to the appropriate interview round and queue. Fail: A student is assigned to an incorrect round or omitted.                            | Organizes the multi-round interview process.                                        |
| FR-005  | Functional  | The system shall allow the Placement Officer to process offer letters and allow students to view the status of their offers.                                | High     | Pass: An offer can be processed and its status is visible to the student. Fail: A processed offer has an incorrect or unavailable status.                          | Provides a structured way to complete and track the placement process.              |
| NFR-001 | Security    | The system shall encrypt all student academic records and offer documents at rest using AES-256 encryption.                                                 | High     | Pass: Stored academic records and offer documents are encrypted using AES-256. Fail: Such stored data is accessible without encryption.                            | Protects sensitive student academic and offer information.                          |
| NFR-002 | Performance | The system shall maintain the target response latency for resume parsing and eligibility filtering under simulated peak placement-cell load.                | High     | Pass: Performance testing under simulated peak load meets the defined target latency. Fail: The target latency is exceeded.                                        | Ensures the system remains responsive during peak usage.                            |
